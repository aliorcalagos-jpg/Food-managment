FLIGHT MANUAL

➤Tap a rocket to launch it the way its chevrons point. Glowing chevrons mean the way out is clear; dark ones mean another rocket is in the way.
◇Hold a rocket to see its flight path and what blocks it. Release to launch, slide off to cancel.
◎Launched rockets land on 5 pads, aim and shoot saucers of their color, front of the wave first.
!Fill every pad with the wrong colors and the hangar jams. Keep the wave off the shield.
⚡From sector 3, every few launches a warp storm shuffles the colors of the hangar and the wave. Watch the meter and plan for it. A storm never makes a sector unwinnable.
★One golden rocket hides in each sector: launch it for +500 and bonus XP before a storm moves it.
⊕+1 Pad, Undo, Shuffle and Radar get you out of trouble. Level up and climb the Star Pass to earn more.
✦Your best stars per sector fill the Star Pass: 30 tiers of boosters, XP and engine trails. Replay sectors for 3 stars to climb faster.
